After you adjust any weight or scenario setting, all three scores display their signed difference from the spreadsheet defaults, including ±0.00 for an unchanged result. When every setting matches the workbook defaults, delta labels are hidden. After any adjustment, zero changes are shown as ±0.00. Use the change-overlay switch in Layers to map per-cell changes; the drawn-area averages remain available here. Results use the exact geometric intersection of each drawn polygon and the exported study-area cells. Each cell’s score is weighted by its overlapping area. When a change is visible, it is the current area mean minus the default area mean (original workbook weights, original Baseline/Gap routing, and 50/50 Combined balance). No-data scores are excluded separately for each metric. These are summaries of the exported grid; they do not recalculate the original ArcGIS rasters.
